Rules & FAQ

Everything you need to know

Clear rules are the foundation of a fair challenge. If your question isn't answered here, our support team is available around the clock.

Ask Our Team
What is a Sovereign Funding Challenge?

An evaluation on a simulated trading account. Reach the profit target without breaching the loss limits, and you become a Sovereign Trader with a funded account and a share of the profits you generate.

How are the daily and maximum loss calculated?

Both are fixed percentages of your starting balance. The daily loss limit resets at 00:00 server time and includes open and closed positions. The maximum loss is static — it does not trail your profits upward.

Is there a time limit to pass?

No. Take as long as you need on every phase. You only need to trade a minimum of 4 days per phase.

How and when do I get paid?

Your first payout can be requested 14 days after your funded account is issued, then every 14 days. Profit splits start at 80% and rise to 90% through the Scaling Plan. Your challenge fee is refunded with your first payout.

Can I trade news and hold over the weekend?

News trading and weekend holding are permitted during the challenge phases. On funded accounts, opening or closing trades within 2 minutes of high-impact news is restricted.

Which instruments can I trade?

Forex majors and minors, global indices, gold and silver, energies and leading cryptocurrencies.

What happens if I break a rule?

The account is closed and the attempt ends. You can start again at any time, and accounts that end in profit qualify for a discounted retry.

Am I trading real money?

No. All challenge and funded accounts are simulated. Profit splits are paid from Sovereign Funding Challenges' own funds based on your simulated performance.
